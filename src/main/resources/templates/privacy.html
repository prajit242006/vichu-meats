Your information

Vichu Meats collects your name, phone number, order choices, requested date, notes and, for delivery, your address. Sheep enquiries also include preferred weight and festival details.

This information is stored in the business database so the owner can respond to requests, arrange fulfilment and keep business records. Only authorised administrators can view order and enquiry lists.

The cart stores product identifiers and quantities in your server session. Session and security cookies are required for cart and form protection. Customer contact details are not stored in browser localStorage.

Google Maps, social links and optional WhatsApp handoff open third-party services governed by their own privacy policies. WhatsApp only sends a message when you press Send.

To ask about access, correction or deletion, contact the owner using details on the contact page or at the supplied location. The owner should review retention practices before launch and delete records no longer needed.
